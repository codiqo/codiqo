/*[- the severity scale, inserted where a sentence or a line needs it: the file ends without a newline -]*/Severity follows certainty and harm, as Codiqo's scoring has always graded it. "blocking": you traced the defect through the code and it will cause incorrect behaviour in normal use: a security hole, data loss or corruption, a race or deadlock, a crash or failed start on a normal path, a build that no longer compiles, a wrong amount of money or a wrong business result. "major": a real defect that needs particular conditions to show, or whose effect is limited and recoverable, or one you strongly suspect but cannot fully confirm because context is missing. "minor": a low-impact edge case. Never lower a traced, certain defect to major because the fix looks small. Judge the harm against what the changed code exists to do, not against the system in general: a defect that defeats the purpose of the change (a watchdog that reports a finished or the wrong migration, a retry that does not retry, a cache that serves stale entries, a metric that counts the wrong thing) is at least "major", even when its only visible effect is a log line or a metric.
